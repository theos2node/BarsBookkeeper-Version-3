# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and keep
technical disagreement focused on the work. Do not publish another person's
private information or operational data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, sustained
disruption, and disclosure of private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may
remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the
community.
